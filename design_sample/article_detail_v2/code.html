The Architecture of Intelligence

The shift from traditional cloud storage to intensive generative AI training requires a fundamental redesign of the industrial floor. No longer are we looking at simple stacks of hardware; we are observing the birth of the Compute Refinery. These facilities process raw electricity and data into high-value intelligence, much like a traditional refinery processes crude into fuel.

Financial analysts must look beyond the chip manufacturers. The real bottleneck—and therefore the real opportunity—lies in the specialized physical infrastructure: heat exchangers, modular power grids, and high-frequency interconnects.
